Attention as an interacting particle system

Numerical demonstrations for Part II. Open the examples below for lecture presentation; all figures, data, and controls work offline.

1. Query sensitivity and rare mass

Move the query, change the temperature, or add a small mass at a preferred key. Compare the query derivative with the much larger amplification of a measure perturbation.

2. Accuracy and stability of time integrators

The solvable consensus system separates time-step accuracy from damping. Euler, RK2, RK4, implicit Euler, and implicit midpoint have different errors and stability limits.

3. Normalized and unnormalized identity attention

Play or scrub the particle trajectories and compare temperatures. These are the raw Euclidean attention equations: the particles can expand while their interaction energy decreases. A stopped unnormalized run marks the displayed radius cutoff, not a stationary solution.
