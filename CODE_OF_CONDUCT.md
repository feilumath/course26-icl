# Code of Conduct

This repository is a shared classroom space. All students, TAs, and the
instructor are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive in comments, issues, and pull requests.
- Give credit where due; do not present others' work (from this repository
  or elsewhere) as your own.
- Keep discussion focused on the course material and its presentation.
- Only modify files that are yours to modify (your own project folder, or
  lecture-note fixes proposed via pull request rather than direct edits).

Violations (harassment, plagiarism, academic dishonesty, etc.) should be
reported to the instructor and will be handled according to the university's
academic integrity and conduct policies.

This document is intentionally short; when in doubt, follow the JHU
[Student Conduct Code](https://studentaffairs.jhu.edu/policies-guidelines/student-code/)
and the course's academic integrity policy stated on the course website.
